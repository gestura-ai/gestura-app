# Gestura.app Community Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment for our community include:

### ✅ Positive Behaviors

- **Being Respectful**: Treating all community members with respect and courtesy
- **Being Inclusive**: Welcoming newcomers and helping them feel part of the community
- **Being Constructive**: Providing helpful feedback and suggestions
- **Being Patient**: Understanding that people have different skill levels and backgrounds
- **Being Collaborative**: Working together towards common goals
- **Being Professional**: Maintaining professional conduct in all interactions
- **Being Empathetic**: Showing understanding and compassion for others
- **Being Open-minded**: Considering different perspectives and approaches
- **Being Supportive**: Helping others learn and grow
- **Being Accountable**: Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them

### ❌ Unacceptable Behaviors

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- **Harassment**: Any form of harassment, intimidation, or bullying
- **Discrimination**: Discriminatory language or actions based on personal characteristics
- **Hate Speech**: Language that attacks or demeans individuals or groups
- **Personal Attacks**: Insulting, derogatory, or offensive comments about individuals
- **Trolling**: Deliberately provocative or disruptive behavior
- **Spam**: Excessive self-promotion or irrelevant content
- **Doxxing**: Publishing private information without consent
- **Sexual Content**: Unwelcome sexual attention or inappropriate sexual content
- **Threats**: Threats of violence or harm, whether physical or otherwise
- **Impersonation**: Pretending to be someone else or misrepresenting affiliations

## Community Guidelines

### Communication

- **Use Clear Language**: Write clearly and avoid jargon when possible
- **Stay On Topic**: Keep discussions relevant to the community's purpose
- **Search Before Posting**: Check if your question has already been answered
- **Use Appropriate Channels**: Post in the correct forum, channel, or repository
- **Be Concise**: Respect others' time by being clear and concise
- **Use Proper Formatting**: Format code, logs, and technical content appropriately

### Technical Discussions

- **Provide Context**: Include relevant details when asking for help
- **Share Solutions**: If you solve a problem, share the solution with others
- **Credit Sources**: Acknowledge code, ideas, or solutions from others
- **Test Before Sharing**: Verify that code examples and solutions work
- **Document Changes**: Explain the reasoning behind suggestions or changes
- **Be Constructive**: Focus on the technical aspects, not personal preferences

### Collaboration

- **Respect Decisions**: Accept project maintainers' decisions gracefully
- **Follow Processes**: Adhere to established contribution guidelines
- **Be Patient**: Understand that reviews and responses may take time
- **Offer Help**: Volunteer to help with tasks within your abilities
- **Share Knowledge**: Contribute to documentation and knowledge sharing
- **Mentor Others**: Help newcomers learn and contribute effectively

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all community spaces, including:

- **GitHub Repositories**: Issues, pull requests, discussions, and wikis
- **Discord Server**: All channels and direct messages
- **Community Forum**: All posts, comments, and private messages
- **Social Media**: Official accounts and community hashtags
- **Events**: Virtual and in-person meetups, conferences, and workshops
- **Email Communications**: Mailing lists and direct communications
- **Other Platforms**: Any platform where community members interact

This Code of Conduct also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders and moderators are responsible for:

- **Clarifying Standards**: Explaining and interpreting community standards
- **Enforcing Guidelines**: Taking appropriate action for violations
- **Fair Treatment**: Ensuring consistent and fair enforcement
- **Privacy Protection**: Maintaining confidentiality of reports when appropriate
- **Continuous Improvement**: Regularly reviewing and updating guidelines

## Reporting Guidelines

### How to Report

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it through one of these channels:

#### Primary Reporting Channels

1. **Email**: conduct@gestura.app
2. **Discord**: Direct message to @Moderator
3. **GitHub**: Use the "Report" feature on issues/comments
4. **Community Forum**: Use the "Report" button on posts

#### Anonymous Reporting

For anonymous reports, use our anonymous reporting form:
- **Web Form**: https://gestura.app/report-anonymous
- **Encrypted Email**: Use our PGP key for sensitive reports

### What to Include

When reporting, please include:

- **Description**: Clear description of what happened
- **Context**: Where and when the incident occurred
- **Evidence**: Screenshots, links, or other relevant evidence
- **Impact**: How the behavior affected you or others
- **Witnesses**: Names of others who witnessed the behavior (if any)
- **Previous Incidents**: Any related previous incidents

### Confidentiality

All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality. We will:

- **Protect Privacy**: Keep reporter identity confidential when possible
- **Limit Access**: Only share information with those who need to know
- **Secure Storage**: Store reports securely and delete when no longer needed
- **No Retaliation**: Protect reporters from retaliation

## Enforcement Actions

Community leaders may take the following actions for violations:

### 1. Warning
**Trigger**: Minor violations or first-time offenses
**Action**: Private warning explaining the violation
**Duration**: Immediate
**Appeal**: Can request clarification

### 2. Temporary Restriction
**Trigger**: Repeated minor violations or moderate violations
**Action**: Temporary restriction from community participation
**Duration**: 1-30 days depending on severity
**Appeal**: Can appeal after 48 hours

### 3. Permanent Ban
**Trigger**: Severe violations or repeated violations after warnings
**Action**: Permanent removal from all community spaces
**Duration**: Permanent
**Appeal**: Can appeal after 6 months with demonstrated change

### 4. Immediate Ban
**Trigger**: Severe violations (threats, doxxing, hate speech)
**Action**: Immediate permanent removal
**Duration**: Permanent
**Appeal**: Limited appeal process for exceptional circumstances

## Appeal Process

If you believe an enforcement action was taken in error:

1. **Submit Appeal**: Email appeals@gestura.app within 30 days
2. **Provide Evidence**: Include any relevant evidence or context
3. **Review Process**: Appeals are reviewed by different moderators
4. **Decision**: Final decision communicated within 14 days
5. **Implementation**: Approved appeals are implemented immediately

## Community Leadership

### Moderators

Our community moderators are volunteers who help maintain a positive environment:

- **Selection**: Chosen based on community involvement and judgment
- **Training**: Receive training on community guidelines and conflict resolution
- **Accountability**: Subject to the same Code of Conduct as all members
- **Support**: Receive support and guidance from community leaders

### Responsibilities

Community leaders and moderators will:

- **Model Behavior**: Demonstrate positive community behavior
- **Enforce Fairly**: Apply guidelines consistently and fairly
- **Communicate Clearly**: Explain decisions and reasoning when appropriate
- **Seek Input**: Consult with other moderators on difficult decisions
- **Continuous Learning**: Stay updated on best practices for community management

## Updates and Changes

This Code of Conduct may be updated periodically to:

- **Reflect Growth**: Adapt to community growth and changes
- **Improve Clarity**: Make guidelines clearer and more specific
- **Address Issues**: Respond to new types of problems or situations
- **Incorporate Feedback**: Include community feedback and suggestions

### Change Process

1. **Proposal**: Changes proposed by community leaders or members
2. **Discussion**: Community discussion period (minimum 14 days)
3. **Feedback**: Collect and review community feedback
4. **Revision**: Revise based on feedback
5. **Approval**: Final approval by community leaders
6. **Implementation**: Changes take effect with 7 days notice

### Notification

Changes will be communicated through:

- **Announcement**: Posted in all community channels
- **Email**: Sent to all registered community members
- **Documentation**: Updated in all relevant documentation
- **Changelog**: Detailed changelog maintained
