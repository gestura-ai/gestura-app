# Aerospace: Mission & Architecture

Use this module when the task is defining a mission profile, allocating system budgets, comparing subsystem options, or recommending an aerospace architecture under real environmental constraints.

## Focus

- Mission profile and environment determine the architecture.
- Mass, power, thermal, communication, and reliability budgets should stay visible.
- Trade studies should expose assumptions, margins, and subsystem coupling clearly.

## High-Value Defaults

- Start from mission profile, environment, and critical constraints.
- Keep mass, power, thermal, and reliability budgets visible.
- Treat avionics, structure, propulsion, and operations as an integrated system.
- Prefer trade studies with explicit assumptions and margins.
- End with the recommended architecture and its main risks.

## Review Lens

- Which mission phases and environments dominate the design?
- Where are the tightest margins in mass, power, thermal, or reliability?
- Which subsystem tradeoffs create the largest system-level ripple effects?
- What assumptions would most likely force architectural rework?

## Common Failure Modes

- Optimizing one subsystem while breaking the overall budget.
- Hiding margin assumptions or environmental constraints.
- Treating operations as separate from architecture.
- Recommending a concept without showing its dominant system risks.

## Good Outputs

- Mission and subsystem trade studies.
- Budget summaries with assumptions and margins.
- Architecture recommendations with dominant risks and couplings.
- Notes on what analysis or test should happen next.

## Retrieval Hints

mission architecture, subsystem trade study, mass budget, power budget, thermal budget, avionics, propulsion, aerospace environment, margin policy.
