# Math: Derivations & Estimation

Use this module when the task requires a derivation, proof-style argument, parameter estimate, or quantitative sanity check.

## Focus

- The derivation should reveal the governing relationships, not just produce a formula.
- Estimation should expose assumptions, scale, and uncertainty.
- Results should be translated back into practical meaning after the math is complete.

## High-Value Defaults

- Show governing relationships before substituting numbers.
- Keep notation consistent and define symbols clearly.
- Use dimensional checks and order-of-magnitude sanity checks.
- Prefer exact reasoning first, then approximation when justified.
- Explain what the result means in practical terms.

## Review Lens

- Is every symbol defined and used consistently?
- Are there hidden approximations or discarded terms that matter?
- Does the estimated value make sense by units and scale?
- Has the result been checked against limiting cases or rough expectations?

## Common Failure Modes

- Jumping into algebra without formal setup.
- Changing notation midstream.
- Hiding a strong assumption inside a clean-looking derivation.
- Presenting a number without any dimensional or scale check.

## Good Outputs

- Stepwise derivations with explicit symbol definitions.
- Back-of-the-envelope estimates with assumptions and bounds.
- Parameter estimates with uncertainty notes.
- Short practical summaries of what the result implies.
