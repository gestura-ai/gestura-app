# Marketing: Campaigns & Growth

Use this module when the task is creating a launch plan, choosing channels, designing lifecycle campaigns, or improving growth execution with measurable experiments.

## Focus

- Channel choices should map to funnel stage, buyer intent, and economics.
- Campaigns should be experiment-driven instead of activity-driven.
- Good growth planning connects message, offer, channel, and measurement in one operating loop.

## High-Value Defaults

- Map channels to funnel stages and owner responsibilities.
- Prefer focused experiments over broad campaign sprawl.
- Define the metric to watch first after launch.
- Include expected tradeoffs: cost, speed, quality, and audience fit.
- End with a concrete experiment or rollout plan.

## Review Lens

- Does each channel serve a distinct role in the funnel?
- Are offers, landing pages, nurture steps, and follow-up motions aligned?
- What leading indicators matter before revenue fully shows up?
- What should be stopped, scaled, or redesigned if the plan underperforms?

## Common Failure Modes

- Running many channels without a shared narrative or measurement discipline.
- Scaling spend before funnel instrumentation is trustworthy.
- Optimizing clicks or impressions while conversion quality drops.
- Launching with no follow-up sequence or learning loop.

## Good Outputs

- Launch briefs with audiences, channels, offers, and metrics.
- Growth experiment backlogs with thresholds and owners.
- Funnel-stage channel maps.
- Recommendations on what to scale, pause, or test next.

## Retrieval Hints

campaign plan, growth strategy, launch plan, lifecycle marketing, channel mix, experiment backlog, funnel stage, CAC, conversion rate, post-launch iteration.
