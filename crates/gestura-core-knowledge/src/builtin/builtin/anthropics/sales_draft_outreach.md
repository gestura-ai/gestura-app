## Principle
Research first, then draft. Avoid generic openers and feature dumps.

## Triggers
- “draft outreach to [person/company]”
- “write cold email to [prospect]”
- “reach out to [name]”

## Steps
1. Identify target (person + role + company; accept LinkedIn URL/domain)
2. Research:
   - company basics + positioning
   - recent news/trigger
   - person background + relevance
3. Pick a hook (priority): trigger event → mutual connection → their content → initiative → role-based pain
4. Draft email with one value prop + one CTA
5. Provide LinkedIn backup message

## Output
```markdown
# Outreach Draft: [Person] @ [Company]

## Research summary
- Hook: ...
- Goal: ...

## Email draft (plain text)
To: [email or unknown]
Subject: [<50 chars]

Hi [Name],

[Personal hook]

[1–2 sentences: their likely challenge + why you can help]

Worth a quick 15 min this week?

Best,
[You]

## LinkedIn connection request (<300 chars)
...
```

## Email style rules
- No markdown formatting
- Short paragraphs
- One ask, low friction
