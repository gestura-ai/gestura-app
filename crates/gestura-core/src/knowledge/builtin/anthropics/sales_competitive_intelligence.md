## Goal
Produce competitor intel suitable for deals:
- where they win / where we win
- positioning + pricing signals
- talk tracks + landmine questions
- (optional) an HTML “battlecard” artifact

## Trigger phrases
- “competitive intel”
- “battlecard for [competitor]”
- “how do we compare to [competitor]”

## Inputs
Required:
- your company/product (1-line)
- competitors (1–5)

Optional:
- specific deal context
- known objections / why competitor came up

## Research workflow
For each competitor:
1. Product + features + target market
2. Pricing model + packaging
3. Recent releases/news (90 days)
4. Reviews (G2/Capterra/etc.)
5. Hiring signals (strategy clues)
6. Summarize “wins/losses” with honest tradeoffs

```markdown

## Summary

- Biggest risk vs competitor: ...

## Per-competitor

**Positioning:** ...

**Where we win:**
- ...
**Talk track:** ...
**Landmine questions:**
1. ...
```
