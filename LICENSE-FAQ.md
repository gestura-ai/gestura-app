## Frequently Asked Questions

### Who can use Gestura for free?

You can use Gestura completely free if you fall into any of these categories:

1. **Personal Use**: Hobbyists, students, researchers, or anyone using Gestura for non-commercial purposes
2. **Non-Profit Organizations**: Charities, educational institutions, research organizations, government entities
3. **Small Businesses**: Any company with less than $1M annual gross revenue
4. **Contributors**: Anyone contributing improvements back to the project
5. **Evaluation**: Any commercial entity for up to 90 days

### How does the revenue threshold work?

- The $1M threshold applies to **total annual gross revenue** of your company
- Revenue from affiliated entities under common ownership is aggregated
- Once you exceed $1M, the 3% contribution applies only to revenue **attributable to Gestura**

### How do I calculate "attributable revenue"?

Revenue is attributable to Gestura if it comes from products or services that:
- Incorporate Gestura directly
- Are enabled by Gestura's functionality
- Substantially depend on Gestura

**Example**: If you build a voice-controlled productivity app using Gestura and earn $5M from it, and 20% of your app's value comes from voice features, you'd contribute 3% × $1M = $30,000.

**Alternative**: If calculating attributable revenue is impractical, you may contribute 0.5% of total annual gross revenue instead.

### When and how do I pay?

1. **When**: Within 90 days of your fiscal year end
2. **First Payment**: After your first full fiscal year of commercial use
3. **How**: 
   - Direct payment to Gestura AI LLC
   - GitHub Sponsors: https://github.com/sponsors/gestura-ai
   - Open Collective: https://opencollective.com/gestura

### What about enterprise licensing?

For organizations preferring predictable costs, we offer:

- **Enterprise License**: Fixed annual fee, no revenue tracking required
- **Partner Program**: Royalty-free licenses for strategic partners
- **OEM License**: Per-unit fees for hardware integration

Contact licensing@gestura.ai for details.

### Can I modify and distribute Gestura?

Yes! You may:
- ✅ View and modify the source code
- ✅ Use it internally for any purpose (subject to license terms)
- ✅ Distribute modifications (with license notice)
- ✅ Contribute improvements back (and use for free)

### How does this compare to other licenses?

| License | Commercial Use | Revenue Sharing | Opens Source |
|---------|---------------|-----------------|--------------|
| MIT/Apache | Unrestricted free | No | Already open |
| GPL/AGPL | Requires sharing code | No | Already open |
| BUSL | Restricted, then free | No | After 4 years |
| FSL | Non-compete, then free | No | After 2 years |
| **Gestura GPL-1.0** | Free <$1M, shared >$1M | Yes (3%) | Source available |

### What if I'm not sure about my obligations?

Contact us at licensing@gestura.ai with questions. We're here to help, not to trap anyone.

### Is this open source?

Gestura is **source-available** but not technically "Open Source" per OSI definition due to the revenue sharing requirement. However:
- Source code is fully visible and auditable
- You can modify and redistribute
- Most users (personal, non-profit, small business) use it entirely free
- Large commercial users contribute to ongoing development
